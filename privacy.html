Privacy Policy

Piti Poti Studio · Effective date: 5 November 2026

Piti Poti Studio ("the app") is a private publishing tool operated by Utku Saygı. It is used only to upload and manage videos on YouTube channels owned by its operator: Peety & Pohty and Sunbeam Sessions. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts of its own.

What data the app accesses

Through Google's OAuth consent, the app requests the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API permissions needed to:

  • upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists and publishing schedules on the operator's own channels;
  • read basic channel information and aggregate performance reports (views, watch time) for those channels.

The app does not access viewers' personal data, comments of individual users, Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google service.

How data is stored

OAuth access tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer and are used solely to perform the actions above. Aggregate analytics may be saved locally to plan future videos. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Children's content

Peety & Pohty videos are marked "Made for Kids" on YouTube. The app never collects any information from children or viewers. Viewer interactions are governed by YouTube's Terms and Google's Privacy Policy.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored tokens are deleted when the app is no longer used.
